## 1. Executive Summary

In Phase 6, comprehensive browser UI acceptance and release-readiness verification was performed across all functional surfaces of the SENTINEL emergency-drill application. Verification covered the Common Operational Picture (Dashboard), FieldLink offline incident reporting and batch synchronization, SkyWatch simulated airspace telemetry and alert acknowledgment, GeoIntel satellite imagery change detection with candidate review and corrupt-image error handling, MediRoute triage with hazard avoidance and disconnected corridor handling, and system-wide deterministic baseline reset.

All verification steps were executed interactively in real Google Chrome (`v156.0.8078.47`) via Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP) and validated with end-to-end regression suites and production bundle builds.

### Demonstration Boundary & Statutory Disclaimers
- **Demonstration Only:** All telemetry, tracks, radar targets, sensor rasters, casualties, vital signs, road networks, and incident queues are synthetic training models for an emergency response drill.
- **No Real Military Intelligence:** The platform does not claim real military threat identification or tactical surveillance capabilities.
- **No Clinically Validated Triage:** The simplified START triage engine is an algorithmic demonstration of rule-based emergency sorting; it does not replace medical triage judgment.
- **No Real Drone Detection:** Radar feeds and kinematic trajectories are generated by a deterministic seeded pseudorandom simulation engine.

## 7. Minimal Bug Fixes Applied in Phase 6

To satisfy acceptance criteria without redesigning the UI:
1. **Responsive Header Badge Collision (`src/app/CommandHeader.tsx`, `src/styles/index.css`):**
   - *Issue:* In narrow mobile viewports (375x667), the center notice badge ("Simulated training data") collided visually with the module title text.
   - *Fix:* Added `className="header-notice-badge"` to the center wrapper in `CommandHeader.tsx` and `.header-notice-badge { display: none !important; }` inside `@media (max-width: 900px)` in `src/styles/index.css`.
2. **MediRoute Disconnected Route Display (`src/modules/mediroute/MediRouteModule.tsx`):**
   - *Issue:* When all roads between origin and destination were blocked, the routing engine correctly returned `routeFound: false`, but the UI right panel statically displayed an "Active" badge with hardcoded distance/ETA text.
   - *Fix:* Updated lines 208 and 535-555 to dynamically check `activeRoute.alternativeFound === false || activeRoute.routeFound === false`. When disconnected, it displays a `Disconnected` badge in critical red, lists `All corridors obstructed`, renders the engine's explanatory message, and displays Mean ETA as `N/A`.
